Your Friend grows a farm.

A greyscale, faded-colour farming life sim in a 2.5D valley you can spin around. Your Rare Friend clears the field, plants, waters and harvests through four seasons, and everyone in town is a Rare Friend too, including the ones in your wallet.

Villagers walking by
What's inside

A whole valley, made of Friends.

A harvest-season life sim built around the Friends in your wallet: their on-chain sprites and families.

✿

Grow through the seasons

Clear weeds and rocks, till, plant, water every day. Ten crops across spring, summer, autumn and winter; some regrow, and out-of-season crops wither when the season turns.

☀

Days, nights and weather

The clock runs 6 am to 2 am. Lamps and windows glow at night, rain and snow water the crops for you, and petals, leaves and snowflakes drift by.

♥

Energy and rest

Every tool costs energy. Get tired and your Friend slows down; stay up past 2 am and you pass out. Eat crops or café food, or go to bed.

⌂

A town of Rare Friends

Eight villagers with schedules, favourite gifts and hearts, plus your own owned Friends, who move in and help water once they like you.

↻

Spin the camera

Rotate the 2.5D valley in quarter turns (Q and R, or the buttons). Clips can slowly orbit the farm on their own.

●

Friend Films

Record a little clip of your farm and save it as a GIF, or as a video with the game's music, then post it to X. Each day also draws a diary card.

Friend Films

Film your farm.

  1. Press ● Clip. Six seconds of your farm, with an optional slow camera orbit and a little label.
  2. Get a GIF from the game's own encoder, and a video with the music where the browser can record it.
  3. Post it to X with the share sheet on phones, or save it and open a prefilled post on desktop.
  4. Every night also draws a diary card of your day, ready to post: @RareFriendsNFT #RareFriends #RareFriendsValley.

Moonlight Blessings.

One blessing costs 1 RF (simulated) and returns 0.88 RF in expected value. Keep it for daily fertilizer, morning dew, better prices or golden crops, or redeem it for its RF value. Every blessing also grants RF décor that boosts the crops around it.
